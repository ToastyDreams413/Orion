Operational meaning
intent, capability, environment, anomaly, final threat level
final_score = (intent × .42 + capability × .32 + environment × .10 + anomaly × .16) × confidence factor

Escalation rules raise high-consequence classes when directness, time pressure, or predicted zone intersection is high.

Current AI / ML components

IsolationForest trained on 800 synthetic normal-track samples; outputs anomaly pressure from speed, directness, TTZ, uncertainty, radiation, thermal, swarm, and transponder features.

RandomForestClassifier trained on 1,040 synthetic examples; predicts benign / suspicious / critical from a 10-feature vector.

KMeans clusters behavior using motion and signal features so tracks can be described by pattern, not just score.

Policy model maps a 19-feature advisor vector to monitor, tracking, identity, patrol, advisory, civil-protection, intercept, or stand-down categories.
